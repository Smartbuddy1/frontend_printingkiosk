Refund Policy

Last updated: Today

1. General Refund Terms

We strive to provide seamless printing experiences. If a print job fails due to a machine error (e.g., paper jam, low ink, software crash), you may be eligible for a refund.

2. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team within 24 hours of the failed transaction. Provide your transaction ID, kiosk location, and time of the issue.

3. Processing Time

Approved refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days, depending on your original payment method and banking institution.

4. Non-Refundable Scenarios

We do not issue refunds for user errors, such as printing the wrong document, selecting incorrect formatting options, or leaving printed documents behind.
