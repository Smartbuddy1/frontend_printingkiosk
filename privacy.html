Privacy Policy

Last updated: Today

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide and improve our printing services. This includes basic account details, transaction records, and temporary storage of documents specifically queued for printing.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used exclusively to facilitate your print jobs, handle billing, and provide customer support. We do not sell or share your personal information with third-party marketers.

3. Document Security

Any documents uploaded to our system are encrypted during transit and at rest. They are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers shortly after your print job is completed or aborted.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about how we handle your privacy or data, please reach out to our support team.
